WHO WE ARE

Engineering capability.
Execution excellence.

Integrated project delivery for the infrastructure that connects, powers and supports communities.

ARCSONS INFRATECH PRIVATE LIMITED is a multidisciplinary EPC and infrastructure project-services company delivering engineering and execution solutions for critical infrastructure.

Our business is built around integrated project delivery across Telecom & OFC Infrastructure, City Gas Distribution, Water & Sewer Infrastructure, Electrical & Power Infrastructure, HDD & Trenchless Technology, Solar EPC and Civil & Utility Infrastructure.

ARCSONS supports projects through the complete lifecycle — from surveys, planning and engineering through procurement, construction, installation, testing, commissioning and final handover.

Our objective is simple: deliver technically sound infrastructure safely, reliably and professionally.
